SkateCart Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SkateCart ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information in connection with the SkateCart services (the "Services"). By using the Services, you consent to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account data: name, email, password hash, role, profile details, and application information you provide.

Product activity: product URLs you submit, links created, clicks, attributed actions, and performance data.

Editorial publishing data: product metadata (such as titles, images, and descriptions) used to generate editorial guides on SkateCart, our editorial publishing platform.

Affiliate Disclosure: SkateCart is a participant in the Walmart Affiliate Program, Impact, and Amazon Associates. We earn a commission for purchases made through our links at no extra cost to you.

Payment data: payout account details you submit, processed by trusted payment partners; we do not store full bank account numbers.

Technical data: device and browser information, IP addresses, and cookies used for security, sessions, and service improvement.

2. How We Use Information

Provide, secure, and improve the Services.

Generate trackable shop links and attribute earnings to your account.

Prepare and publish editorial product guides on SkateCart, including affiliate disclosures and shop buttons linked to your promotions.

Communicate account notices, application status, and transactional emails.

Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and policy violations.

Comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We share data with service providers solely to operate the Services, including:

  • Affiliate networks and retailers for link tracking and commission attribution.
  • Hosting providers (GitHub Pages) and related publishing infrastructure to host public editorial guides.
  • Email and payment providers for communications and payouts.

Editorial guides published on SkateCart are public and may include product information derived from links you create. We may disclose information to comply with law or protect rights and safety. We do not sell personal data.

4. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the Services and meet legal and accounting obligations. Published editorial guides may remain publicly available after account closure unless removal is required by law or requested where applicable.

5. Your Choices

Access, update, or delete certain account information in your settings.

Unsubscribe from non-essential communications where applicable.

Disable cookies via browser settings (may impact functionality).

Contact us with privacy requests as described below.

6. Security

We implement administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. We take steps to protect your information in accordance with this Policy wherever it is processed.

8. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@skatecart.com
