About Us - SkateCart

Welcome to SkateCart! SkateCart is an editorial product discovery platform where we publish independent-style shopping guides and deals for our community.

Our platform empowers authorized creators to generate trackable shop links. When a creator shares a product, our editorial team may publish a curated guide on SkateCart.com, featuring the product information, shopping insights, and an official shop link.

What We Do:
- We curate trending products from top retailers like Walmart, Amazon, and others.
- We create easy-to-share editorial guides that help users discover the best products.
- We work with leading affiliate networks to ensure accurate tracking and attribution.

How We Earn:
SkateCart earns a small commission when you buy through our shop links, at no extra cost to you. This supports our platform and creators. We always include clear affiliate disclosures as per FTC guidelines.

Our mission is to make online shopping transparent, helpful, and rewarding for everyone.
